Robotics & event-based vision

Adam Hines.

I’m a Research Fellow at the QUT Centre for Robotics, working on vision-based positioning for Roo-ver, Australia’s first lunar rover. My research focuses on event-based vision, robotic localisation, and open-source tools.

Based in Brisbane, Australia. Previously at Macquarie University and a Fulbright Scholar at the University of Pennsylvania.
